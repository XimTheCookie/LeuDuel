Privacy Policy – LeuDuel

Last updated: 10/10/2025

LeuDuel is a free, open-source Yu-Gi-Oh! duel companion app for Android, developed as a hobby project. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user data.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

LeuDuel does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or sensitive user data on any server. The developer has no access to any information from your device.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your device using Capacitor Preferences (key-value storage) and never leaves it:

  • Application preferences and settings
  • Duel state (life points, timers, win counters)
  • Duelist profiles (custom names and associated card images)

This data can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data from your device settings.

3. Data Sent to Third Parties

The only external network communication occurs when you use the Card Search feature. In that case, the card name or search query you type is sent to the YGO Pro API (ygoprodeck.com) to retrieve card information.

  • Data sent: card name / search query only
  • Data received: card name, type, description, and image URLs
  • No personal or device-identifying information is ever included in these requests
  • Card data is fetched on demand and not persisted locally
  • Card images are loaded directly from the YGO Pro API and not stored by the app

Please refer to the YGO Pro API terms and privacy policy at https://ygoprodeck.com/api-guide/.

4. Permissions

The app may request the following Android permissions:

  • WAKE_LOCK – to keep the screen on during a duel (Keep Awake feature)
  • INTERNET – solely for the Card Search feature described above

No other sensitive permissions are requested.

5. Children's Privacy

LeuDuel is not directed at children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
